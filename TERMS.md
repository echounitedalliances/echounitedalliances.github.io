# Terms of Service — Resonance accounts

*Last updated 9 September 2026.*

These terms cover creating and using a **Resonance** account on
echounitedalliances.github.io. If you only browse the site — searching
carriers, looking at the network, reading About — none of this applies to
you; a Resonance account is optional and this page only matters once you
make one.

For how members are expected to behave towards each other, and how the
alliance itself is run, see the [Code of Conduct](/#/conduct) instead. These
two documents cover different things: that one is about people, this one is
about the account system.

## What Echo United Alliances is

Echo United Alliances is a virtual airline group in *The Airline Simulator*,
run by its members as a hobby. It is not a company, and this site is not
operated on behalf of one. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
connected to *The Airline Simulator*, any real airline, or any company whose
name a member has borrowed for a fictional carrier.

Nothing on this site involves real money, and no booking made here entitles
anyone to real travel of any kind. Every schedule, fare, and reservation
describes flights inside a game.

## Who can make an account

You must be at least 13 years old, matching Discord's own minimum age —
membership in the alliance itself happens through Discord, and a Resonance
account is meant for people who are already, or intend to be, part of that.

## Creating an account

A Resonance account needs an email address and a password. That's the whole
signup: no real name, no address, no age, no payment details. Your password
is sent straight to Supabase, the service that hosts this site's database
and handles sign-in — it never touches this site's own code, and is never
visible to anyone running the site.

You're responsible for:

- keeping your password to yourself, and using a password you don't reuse
  somewhere that actually matters;
- one account belonging to you — don't create one to impersonate someone
  else, or to act on behalf of an airline that isn't yours;
- anything entered under your account, including bookings and profile
  details, being something you actually meant to submit.

## What an account is for

Signed in, you can:

- save a display name, a home division, and a home airport to your profile
  (all optional, and all editable any time);
- book fictional flights and see them collected under "My trips" instead of
  scattered across a browser's history;
- cancel a booking you made.

None of this is required to use the site. A guest can search the network
and book a flight without ever signing in — an account only adds a place
for that booking to be remembered.

## Acceptable use

Don't:

- try to access another Resonant's account, bookings, or profile;
- automate requests against this site or its database beyond what a person
  clicking around would generate;
- use a booking, a profile field, or anything else on this site to
  harass, impersonate, or misrepresent yourself as someone else;
- try to exploit a bug in the site or its database for advantage.

The Code of Conduct governs everything else about how members treat each
other, on this site or off it.

## Suspension and removal

Because a Resonance account exists to serve the alliance's own membership,
a board member can suspend or remove an account that's being used to
violate these terms or the Code of Conduct. The usual path is the same one
the Code of Conduct describes for anything else: a warning first, and
removal only if the problem continues or was serious on its own.

You can ask for your own account to be deleted at any time, for any reason
or none — see the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md) for how.

## No warranty

This site is run by volunteers, for free, as a hobby project. It's provided
as-is, with no guarantee it will be available, error-free, or that data in
it (including your bookings) will never be lost. There's no service level
to promise and no company standing behind one. If something breaks, tell us
on Discord and we'll do what a hobby project can — but we can't offer more
than that.

## Changes

These terms change when the way Resonance accounts work changes, in the
same commit as the code that changed. The date at the top is when that last
happened.

## Questions
